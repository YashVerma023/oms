Max loss rules

Max loss = allocation × the multiplier for the account's algo in the day's DTE mode. An allocation of 10,000 at 2 gives a max loss of 20,000. These apply to accounts running from today; accounts that already ran take their max loss from the uploaded Max Loss sheet instead. Leave a cell empty where an algo has no rule — those accounts are left alone rather than set to zero.

Multiplier bands

For the single-tag steps only. First step is the allocation at which the multiplier reaches 2; width is each band after that. Edge decides where an exact boundary falls — up gives 3,00,000 a 3, down gives 2,50,000 a 2. The two steps deliberately differ today. The 0DTE series does not use these: its bucket is the Setup rounding basis ÷ 100.
